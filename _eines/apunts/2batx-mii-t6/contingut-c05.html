⟪Arrels i inverses com a potències: \((x^n)'=nx^{n-1}\) val per a qualsevol exponent real. Escriu abans \(\sqrt[m]{x^k}=x^{k/m}\) i \(\frac{1}{x^k}=x^{-k}\). En particular, \(\left(\sqrt x\right)'=\frac{1}{2\sqrt x}\) y \(\left(\frac1x\right)'=-\frac{1}{x^2}\).‖Roots and reciprocals as powers: \((x^n)'=nx^{n-1}\) holds for any real exponent. First write \(\sqrt[m]{x^k}=x^{k/m}\) and \(\frac{1}{x^k}=x^{-k}\). In particular, \(\left(\sqrt x\right)'=\frac{1}{2\sqrt x}\) and \(\left(\frac1x\right)'=-\frac{1}{x^2}\).⟫

⟪D'on ixen les derivades de les inverses trigonomètriques? Si \(y=\arcsin x\), aleshores \(\sin y=x\). Derivant implícitament (apartat 6.8): \(\cos y\cdot y'=1\Rightarrow y'=\frac{1}{\cos y}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin^2y}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\).‖¿De dónde salen las derivadas de las inversas trigonométricas? Si \(y=\arcsin x\), entonces \(\sin y=x\). Derivando implícitamente (apartado 6.8): \(\cos y\cdot y'=1\Rightarrow y'=\frac{1}{\cos y}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin^2y}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\).‖Where do the derivatives of the inverse trigonometric functions come from? If \(y=\arcsin x\), then \(\sin y=x\). Differentiating implicitly (section 6.8): \(\cos y\cdot y'=1\Rightarrow y'=\frac{1}{\cos y}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin^2y}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\).⟫

✎ ⟪Exemples‖Ejemplos‖Examples⟫
  1. \(f(x)=\sqrt[3]{x^2}=x^{2/3}\ \Rightarrow\ f'(x)=\tfrac23x^{-1/3}=\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt[3]{x}}\); \(\quad g(x)=\dfrac{4}{x^3}=4x^{-3}\Rightarrow g'(x)=\dfrac{-12}{x^4}\)
  2. \(f(x)=3^x+\log_2x\ \Rightarrow\ f'(x)=3^x\ln3+\dfrac{1}{x\ln2}\)
  3. \(f(x)=e^x(x^2-2x+2)\ \Rightarrow\ f'(x)=e^x(x^2-2x+2)+e^x(2x-2)=x^2e^x\)
  4. \(f(x)=\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\ \Rightarrow\ f'(x)=\dfrac{\frac1x\cdot x-\ln x}{x^2}=\dfrac{1-\ln x}{x^2}\)
  5. \(f(x)=\dfrac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}\ \Rightarrow\ f'(x)=\dfrac{\cos x(1+\cos x)+\sin^2x}{(1+\cos x)^2}=\dfrac{1+\cos x}{(1+\cos x)^2}=\dfrac{1}{1+\cos x}\)
  6. ⟪La derivada de la tangent:‖La derivada de la tangente:‖The derivative of the tangent:⟫ \(\left(\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}\right)'=\dfrac{\cos^2x+\sin^2x}{\cos^2x}=\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}=1+\tan^2x\)
