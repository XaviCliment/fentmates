⟪Integrals immediates‖Integrales inmediatas‖Standard integrals⟫

⟪Taula d'integrals immediates (es llig derivant la columna de la dreta)‖Tabla de integrales inmediatas (se lee derivando la columna de la derecha)‖Table of standard integrals (read it by differentiating the right-hand column)⟫
\(\displaystyle\int k\,dx=kx+C\)\(\displaystyle\int x^n\,dx=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\quad(n\neq-1)\)
\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|+C\)\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{2\sqrt x}=\sqrt x+C\)
\(\displaystyle\int e^x\,dx=e^x+C\)\(\displaystyle\int a^x\,dx=\frac{a^x}{\ln a}+C\)
\(\displaystyle\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C\)\(\displaystyle\int\cos x\,dx=\sin x+C\)
\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\cos^2x}=\int\left(1+\tan^2x\right)dx=\tan x+C\)\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sin^2x}=-\frac{1}{\tan x}+C\)
\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{1+x^2}=\arctan x+C\)\(\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x+C\)

⟪Moltes integrals s'hi redueixen operant abans: repartint un quocient (\(\frac{x^2+1}{x}=x+\frac1x\)), escrivint les arrels com a potències (\(\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]x}=x^{-1/3}\)) o fent servir identitats trigonomètriques: \(\tan^2x=\frac{1}{\cos^2x}-1\), \(\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}\), \(\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}\).‖Muchas integrales se reducen a ellas operando antes: repartiendo un cociente (\(\frac{x^2+1}{x}=x+\frac1x\)), escribiendo las raíces como potencias (\(\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]x}=x^{-1/3}\)) o usando identidades trigonométricas: \(\tan^2x=\frac{1}{\cos^2x}-1\), \(\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}\), \(\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}\).‖Many integrals reduce to these after some algebra: splitting a quotient (\(\frac{x^2+1}{x}=x+\frac1x\)), writing roots as powers (\(\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]x}=x^{-1/3}\)) or using trigonometric identities: \(\tan^2x=\frac{1}{\cos^2x}-1\), \(\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}\), \(\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}\).⟫

  2. \(\displaystyle\int\frac{\left(x^2+1\right)^2}{x}\,dx\)

    ⟪Desenvolupem i repartim:‖Desarrollamos y repartimos:‖Expand and split:⟫ \(\dfrac{x^4+2x^2+1}{x}=x^3+2x+\dfrac1x\).

    \(\displaystyle\int\left(x^3+2x+\frac1x\right)dx=\frac{x^4}{4}+x^2+\ln|x|+C\).

  3. \(\displaystyle\int\left(\sqrt x+\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x}}\right)dx=\int\left(x^{1/2}+x^{-1/3}\right)dx=\frac{2}{3}x^{3/2}+\frac32x^{2/3}+C=\frac23\sqrt{x^3}+\frac32\sqrt[3]{x^2}+C\)
  4. \(\displaystyle\int\tan^2x\,dx=\int\left(\frac{1}{\cos^2x}-1\right)dx=\tan x-x+C\) ⟪i‖y‖and⟫ \(\displaystyle\int\sin^2x\,dx=\int\frac{1-\cos2x}{2}\,dx=\frac x2-\frac{\sin2x}{4}+C\).
  5. PAU Castella-la Manxa 2024\(\displaystyle\int\frac{2x^2}{x^2+1}\,dx\)

    \(\dfrac{2x^2}{x^2+1}=\dfrac{2\left(x^2+1\right)-2}{x^2+1}=2-\dfrac{2}{x^2+1}\).

    \(\displaystyle\int\left(2-\frac{2}{1+x^2}\right)dx=2x-2\arctan x+C=2(x-\arctan x)+C\).
